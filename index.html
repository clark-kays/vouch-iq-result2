Vouch Recommendation

The five-policy program has adequate breadth for Curebase's stage, but carries three critical structural gaps: (1) the Medical Professional Services Exclusion (EMB-H60801-1022) leaves the company's core DCT platform activities entirely uninsured for professional liability; (2) the D&O/EPL policy expires July 24, 2025 — over two months before the rest of the program — creating an imminent management liability gap; and (3) Social Engineering is explicitly excluded from the Crime policy and capped at $500K net from cyber, leaving BEC fraud materially underinsured.

The CGL/Umbrella tower is adequate, but the Technology Services Limitation (SU 20 85 10 18) means tech E&O claims cannot flow to the umbrella. The Crime policy structure is strong for internal theft and direct wire fraud, but the SE exclusion is a critical gap. The D&O Side A structure ($2M + $1M Additional) is solid. The EPL base retention of $75K is above benchmark, and the High Wage Earner endorsement doubles it to $150K for most of the engineering and clinical staff.

1 URGENT: Renew D&O/EPL by July 24, 2025 — do not allow lapse
D&O/EPL (EM3EII-AX-002298-01) expires July 24, 2025 — claims-made, so any gap period leaves directors personally exposed. Begin marketing by May 24, 2025. Align anniversary date to October 7.
2 Replace Tech E&O with a Life Sciences form that covers clinical trial technology services
EMB-H60801-1022 excludes lab testing, case management, and clinical services — Curebase's core DCT platform activities. Replace with a Life Sciences Tech E&O form (Corix CEM or equivalent) that covers clinical trial technology services without this exclusion.
3 Address the Social Engineering gap: Crime excludes SE, Cyber caps it at $500K
Crime IA C explicitly excludes Social Engineering; Cyber IA 9 caps SE+FTF at $500K combined. Net program SE coverage: $450K after $50K retention. A single pharma sponsor wire transfer can exceed this limit. Increase Cyber SE+FTF sublimit to $5M and add an SE endorsement to the Crime policy, or replace with a Crime form that covers SE.
4 Negotiate EPL retention down from $75K (and $150K for high earners)
Base EPL retention of $75K is above benchmark ($25K–$50K). The High Wage Earner endorsement (EMB-H41201-1222) doubles it to $150K for employees earning over $150K — covering most of the likely claimant population. At $150K retention, most EPL claims will be substantially self-insured. Negotiate base retention to $50K and remove the High Wage Earner endorsement at renewal.
5 Confirm HIPAA regulatory coverage and add explicit HIPAA/HITECH trigger language
The Regulatory Action definition names GDPR and CCPA but omits HIPAA/HITECH. HHS OCR enforcement is Curebase's primary regulatory risk as a Business Associate. Endorse the form to explicitly cover HIPAA/HITECH investigations and penalties.
D&O renewal is urgent. Let's act before July 24, 2025.
